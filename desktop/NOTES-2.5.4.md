# v2.5.4 — Duke Nukem 3D: Legacy Edition, and Duke cover art

**Duke Nukem 3D: Legacy Edition 2.0.8** joins the roster — the overhaul
with dashing, weapon alt-fires, boss challenges and re-tuned enemies.
The mod ships its own customized EDuke32 build (it refuses to run on
anything else), which the launcher uses directly; it auto-loads the
LegacyE directory and plays off the Atomic GRP already in the pack.
Multiplayer rides the same relay path as the other Dukes. The payload
(~4,900 files, 0.86 GB) comes from the bucket on first launch like every
other download.

Also: the Duke cards finally have their cover art — Duke Nukem 64 and
KickAss Duke now show real covers instead of the generated poster.

On the Doom side, **Fate: A Doom Total Conversion** takes the slot
Recurring Nightmare used to hold — its own ZScript and a C1L1 start, so
the launcher drops you straight into the first map. And the Auger;Zenith
cover art is back: the two "lost" images were Doom patch lumps being read
as raw RGB, and re-converting them through each WAD's PLAYPAL recovered
them at full 320x200.
